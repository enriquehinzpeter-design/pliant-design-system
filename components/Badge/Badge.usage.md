Count or presence overlay — unread notifications, pending approvals on a nav item.

```jsx
<Badge badgeContent={7} color="error"><Icon name="Bell" size={24} /></Badge>
<Badge variant="dot" color="info"><Icon name="Receipt" size={24} /></Badge>
```

Zero and null counts hide automatically. `color="secondary"` is overridden in Pliant to the badge token pair (pale yellow fill, brown content) rather than the lime brand colour.
