Identity marker for cardholders, approvers and team members.

```jsx
<Avatar size="small">JS</Avatar>
<AvatarGroup max={3}>
  <Avatar>JS</Avatar><Avatar>MK</Avatar><Avatar>AL</Avatar><Avatar>TR</Avatar>
</AvatarGroup>
```

Initials fall back to the warm contrast surface (#f5f5f0) with 500-weight Geist — Pliant does not use coloured identity backgrounds by default.
