The only way text should be sized in a Pliant surface — every role comes from `designTokens.typography`.

```jsx
<Typography variant="h5">Card overview</Typography>
<Typography variant="body2" color="text.secondary">Issued 12 Mar 2026</Typography>
<Typography variant="overline2" color="text.secondary">Limit</Typography>
```

- `caption2`, `overline2` are Pliant additions to the MUI scale (10px).
- `overline2` is uppercased; `overline` is not.
